Original Drawings · Erik Salander 1920

The original drawings for the 95 sqm Skerry Cruiser were produced by designer Erik Salander in Stockholm in January 1920, at a scale of 1:50. Three sheets survive — rig, accommodation plan, and structural construction — and form the complete basis for the CAD reconstruction work.
